Versatile & Delicious

How To Enjoy Pecans in Your Daily Indian Routine

01
The Morning Power Bowl:

Toss 5–6 roughly crushed pecan halves over your morning oats, muesli, or Greek dahi with a drizzle of honey. It adds a buttery crunch without any sour or bitter tang.

02
The 4 PM Chai Companion:

Lightly dry-roast pecans in a pan with a pinch of sendha namak (rock salt). Pairs majestically with adrak-elachi chai and keeps your evening cravings satisfied until dinner.

03
The Modern Kheer & Halwa Twist:

Swap chopped walnuts or regular nuts in rice kheer, moong dal halwa, or besan laddoos. Pecans infuse a warm toffee-caramel aroma that delights guests.

04
Guilt-Free Midnight Fuel for Fitness:

Keep a jar by your desk. Because net carbs are extremely low, it sustains ketone levels and prevents blood sugar crashes during late-night work.

Everything You Need to Know

Transparent answers regarding our sourcing, taste, and pan-India delivery.

Why should I choose Pecan Nuts over regular Walnuts? +

While both nuts look somewhat similar, their taste and nutrient profile are completely different. Walnuts contain astringent tannins in their skin that leave a dry, bitter aftertaste. Pecan nuts have zero bitterness; they possess a smooth, naturally sweet, buttery, caramel-like richness. Furthermore, Pecans rank #1 in antioxidant capacity (ORAC 17,940) among all tree nuts and have lower net carbs, making them friendlier for children, elders, and keto/diabetic routines.

Are Planet 12 dry fruits raw, roasted, or salted? +

Our standard collection is 100% Raw, Unsalted, and Unadulterated, straight from the orchard harvest. This preserves the live enzymes, essential fatty acids, and clean natural sweetness. You can enjoy them raw or toast them lightly at home with your favorite spices.

What does "W180" mean on your cashew nuts? +

In cashew grading, "W" stands for Whole (unbroken) and the number indicates how many cashew kernels weigh one pound (453g). Lower numbers mean significantly larger nuts. Standard store-bought cashews are typically W320 or W240. W180 is known worldwide as the "King of Cashews"—the largest, plumpest, rarest grade available on the market.

How should I store these nuts in humid Indian weather? +

Our pouches come with a heavy-duty airtight zip-lock seal. For daily use, keep the pouch zipped in a cool, dark pantry. In extreme summer heat or monsoon humidity, storing the pouch (or an airtight glass jar) in the refrigerator maintains that mouthwatering crisp crunch for up to 9–12 months!
